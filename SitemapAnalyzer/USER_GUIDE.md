# SEO Crawler User Guide

## What is the SEO Crawler?

The SEO Crawler analyzes your website's SEO performance by checking every page listed in your sitemap. It identifies issues like missing titles, poor meta descriptions, slow loading pages, and content problems that could hurt your search rankings.

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Get Your Access Code
When you first open the app, you'll see an 8-character access code in the sidebar (like `a1b2c3d4`). **Copy and save this code** - you'll need it to see your results later.

### Step 2: Enter Your Sitemap
1. Find your website's sitemap URL (usually `yoursite.com/sitemap.xml`)
2. Paste it in the "Sitemap URL" field
3. Adjust the delay if needed (3 seconds is fine for most sites)

### Step 3: Start Crawling
Click "Start SEO Crawl" and wait. The app will check every page in your sitemap - this can take a few minutes depending on your site size.

### Step 4: Review Results
Once complete, you'll see three tabs with your results:
- **Detailed Data**: All pages with their SEO scores
- **Key Issues**: Problems that need fixing
- **Page Speed**: How fast your pages load

## Understanding Your Results

### Detailed Data Tab
This shows every page with key metrics:
- **Title**: Should be 30-60 characters, unique for each page
- **Meta Description**: Should be 120-160 characters, compelling
- **Word Count**: Aim for 300+ words for main content pages
- **Load Time**: Target under 3 seconds
- **Status Code**: 200 is good, 404 means broken

### Key Issues Tab
Problems are grouped into:
- **Content Issues**: Missing descriptions, thin content, readability problems
- **Technical Issues**: Missing titles, slow pages, broken links

Focus on fixing issues marked as "Critical" first.

### Page Speed Tab
Visual charts showing:
- How many pages load in different time ranges
- Which HTTP status codes your pages return
- Overall site performance trends

## Saving and Accessing Your Data

### Save Your Access Code
Your 8-character code (shown in the sidebar) is your key to accessing your data later. Save it by:
- Taking a screenshot
- Writing it in your notes app
- Emailing it to yourself
- Saving it in your password manager

### Return to Your Data
From any device or browser:
1. Open the SEO Crawler
2. In the sidebar, find "Returning User?"
3. Enter your saved access code
4. Click "Load My History"

### Export Your Results
Click the "Export to CSV" button to download your data. You can then:
- Open in Excel or Google Sheets
- Share with your team
- Track changes over time
- Create custom reports

## Best Practices

### Crawling Etiquette
- Use 3-5 second delays for large sites
- Don't crawl the same site multiple times per day
- Avoid crawling during peak traffic hours
- Respect the website's server resources

### Interpreting Results
- **Missing Titles**: Fix immediately - critical for SEO
- **No Meta Description**: High priority - affects click-through rates
- **Slow Load Times**: Impacts user experience and rankings
- **Low Word Count**: May indicate thin content
- **High Readability Score**: Easier for users to understand (aim for 60+)

### Taking Action
1. **Start with Critical Issues**: Missing titles, 404 errors, very slow pages
2. **Fix Content Issues**: Add meta descriptions, improve thin content
3. **Optimize Performance**: Address slow-loading pages
4. **Monitor Progress**: Re-crawl monthly to track improvements

## Troubleshooting

### "Invalid sitemap URL"
- Check the URL works in your browser
- Make sure it ends with `.xml`
- Try adding `/sitemap.xml` to your domain
- Some sites use `/sitemap_index.xml`

### Crawl Seems Stuck
- Check your internet connection
- Try reducing the delay setting
- Some large sites take 30+ minutes to crawl completely

### Can't Find My Previous Results
- Double-check your access code for typos
- Access codes are case-sensitive
- If lost, generate a new code and start fresh

### Export Not Working
- Disable popup blockers
- Try a different browser
- Check if your download folder is full

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I crawl my site?**
A: Monthly for active sites, after any major updates, or when you suspect SEO issues.

**Q: What's a good readability score?**
A: 60+ is good, 70+ is excellent. Higher numbers mean easier to read.

**Q: Why are some pages missing from results?**
A: They might not be in your sitemap, return errors, or be blocked by robots.txt.

**Q: Can I crawl competitor sites?**
A: Yes, but be respectful and don't overload their servers.

**Q: How do I fix technical SEO issues?**
A: Work with a developer or use an SEO plugin if you're on WordPress.
